# Ellai operational schedule configuration

The enrichment, quality, follow-up, organization, and correlation schedulers use `CadenceHours` (4 or 8), `PolicyVersion`, and a positive `ConfigurationRevision`. Configuration is scoped by the worker's explicitly configured workspace and organization lists; each scheduler is disabled by default. These defaults describe source configuration, not evidence that a worker is running.

| Role | Default cadence | Default batch | Work per due sweep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ops.enrichment` | 4 hours | 100 | At most one classification job per eligible organization, with up to 100 recent revisions per job. |
| `ops.quality` | 4 hours | 10 | At most 10 work turns per workspace. |
| `ops.followup` | 4 hours | 10 | At most 10 work turns per workspace. |
| `ops.organization` | 8 hours | 10 | At most 10 work turns per workspace. |
| `ops.correlation` | 8 hours | 10 | At most 10 work turns per workspace. |

`BatchSize` is configurable from 1 to 100. For quality, follow-up, organization, and correlation, a sweep that reaches its batch limit advances to the next cadence; remaining work waits for a later sweep. Failed or cancelled sweeps leave the due cursor unchanged for recovery. The deterministic sweeps do not invoke the reasoning model. Enrichment creates jobs but does not itself run classification inference; classification worker capacity and its separate discovery switch govern consumption. Scheduled spatial analysis is not implemented.

Quality schedules fingerprint the policy version, organization scope, and quality rules version. Enrichment schedules fingerprint the policy version, organization scope, worker identity, classification profile, and batch size. Deploy fingerprint-aware workers to every replica and allow old workers to drain before increasing each affected configuration revision; existing schedules created by older versions require a higher revision. Keep these settings identical across replicas and increase the relevant revision whenever they change.

Apply the operational schedule configuration revision migration before deploying workers that use it. Deploy the revision-aware worker version to all replicas before increasing a configuration revision: older binaries do not enforce the revision when claiming work. Do not enable new settings until the old workers have drained.

Apply both enrichment provenance migrations before deploying the updated Gateway: case timeline reads and classification claims use their tables even when enrichment scheduling is disabled. Jobs created before those migrations have no recorded enrichment provenance and do not acquire a retrospective timeline entry.

Apply the clarification proposals and clarification reviews migrations before deploying the updated Gateway because case timeline reads use their tables even when proposals are disabled. `Ellai:Operational:FollowUp:ClarificationFindingCodes` is an optional organization-to-list map. Each organization must also appear in the follow-up `Organizations` scope. Supported values are `occurrence-not-known`, `region-not-known`, `incomplete-coordinate-pair`, and `text-evidence-unavailable`; the default map is empty. A proposal is a generic staff-visible question, not a message to the reporter, a diagnosis, or a change to the report. The same finding is proposed at most once per source revision. Authorized staff can mark a current proposal reviewed; that does not approve contact. Accept/reject decisions and outbound consent workflows are not implemented by this path.

Follow-up schedules fingerprint the policy version, organization scope, and clarification rules. Replicas with different rules at the same configuration revision cannot claim the same schedule. Deploy the fingerprint-aware Gateway to all replicas and allow older workers to drain before increasing the follow-up configuration revision; existing schedules created by older versions need a higher revision to adopt the fingerprint. Use identical settings across replicas and increase the revision whenever scope or rules change.

Follow-up and clarification recommendations are shown as historical after their policy changes or the case leaves the 30-day reporting window. New reviews require a matching current schedule and hold a shared policy lock through the review transaction; historical reviews remain recorded.

Apply the organization proposals and organization decisions migrations before deploying the updated Gateway because case timeline reads their tables even when the worker is disabled. `Ellai:Operational:Organization` is disabled by default. When enabled, configure explicit `Workspaces`, `PolicyVersion`, `ConfigurationRevision`, and `SectorQueues`, a map of organization IDs to sector-to-queue-code mappings. The default cadence is eight hours. Configure identical rules on every replica and change the policy version and configuration revision when rules change. The schedule stores a fingerprint of the policy and rules, and rejects a replica whose rules differ at the same revision. The worker creates suggestions for cases received in the last 30 days, newest first; authorized staff may accept or reject a current suggestion with a protected reason. A decision does not assign, merge, contact anyone, or make a clinical decision. Actual queue assignment and a UI to retrieve review reasons are not yet implemented.

Queue suggestions from a previous policy remain in the timeline as historical. A new decision requires the proposal's policy to match the current organization schedule; the decision holds a shared policy lock until it commits.

To change a cadence or policy, increase the revision for that role on every replica and apply identical settings. A newer revision waits for any active lease to finish or expire, fences the old lease, and makes the schedule due. A replica with an older revision cannot overwrite or claim the newer schedule. Reusing a revision with different settings produces a mismatch warning and does not run the sweep. A policy version change does not itself prove that every previously processed artifact has been reassessed.

The migration refuses rollback while any schedule has a revision greater than 1. Retain the schedule data and use a forward correction for configuration mistakes.

The classification worker may opt in to discovery of existing running backfill jobs with `CLASSIFICATION_AUTOMATIC_BACKFILL_ENABLED=true`. Discovery is restricted to its configured worker identity, current classification profile, allowed organizations, and active workspace-organization links. The worker processes at most one background item per polling interval using the existing result lease and model semaphore. It can also process manually created running jobs in that same scope. Leave it disabled until that behavior and the background capacity are intended for the deployment.

`Ellai:Operational:Correlation` is disabled by default. Its bounded background scan requires the correlation migration, explicit `Workspaces`, `Organizations`, `PolicyVersion`, `ConfigurationRevision`, and `MaximumSeparationDays` (1 to 30). It stores unconfirmed candidates only when recent cases in the same organization and workspace share a sector and region within the configured interval. It never creates a case link. The authenticated case-candidate endpoint rechecks current read access to both cases and current source revisions. The case detail view shows accessible candidates and allows authorized staff to accept or reject them with a protected reason; this does not merge or reassign either case. The paged read scans at most 1,000 candidates per request and returns a continuation only after finding another authorized candidate; the legacy read remains limited to 100 candidates. Source-identifier matching and Qdrant similarity are not yet implemented. Apply the correlation decisions migration before deploying the updated read path. Keep the worker disabled until those workflows and capacity have been assessed. Schedule policy fingerprints include the organization scope and matching interval.

Current-candidate reads and new review decisions also require the candidate's policy fingerprint to match the current correlation schedule. After a policy revision, older suggestions remain stored as history but are not presented or newly reviewed as current. A matching schedule is required even if candidate records exist.

`Ellai:Operational:Enrichment` is disabled by default. When enabled, it requires the same bounded workspace list, policy version, cadence, and configuration revision as the other operational schedulers. On each due run it creates at most one classification recovery job per active, explicitly allowed organization, with at most `BatchSize` (1 to 100) recent case revisions. Completed or failed classifications with a matching base profile and current translation approval, and pending matching backfill items, are excluded. A due run that finds no work does not retain an empty job. Enable automatic backfill discovery separately to process those jobs. A case timeline shows completed classification work only when a recorded scheduled-job lease produced that exact result, or records a reused result when the scheduled turn actually attached it to the case. Failed or uncommitted attempts do not claim completion. This is classification recovery, not the complete enrichment role: location resolution and reasoning-based follow-up proposals remain outstanding.

Apply the classification backfill expiry migration before deploying this Gateway. Pending items that leave the 30-day window are counted as expired; active result leases can finish and completed outcomes retain their actual status. Drain older Gateway replicas before enabling the updated workers, because older replicas do not enforce the claim-time window.

The authenticated `/api/v1/cases/spatial/owned-regions` read ranks unsuppressed administrative region counts for the current officer's readable, owned cases created in the last 30 days. It rejects truncated underlying aggregates. Ellai can invoke the same bounded read through `ellai_owned_region_counts` or the `ops.spatial` interactive delegate in an authorized workspace session; publication and history replay recheck current access. The delegate is enabled by default through `Ellai:Specialists:Spatial:Enabled`. This does not implement the scheduled spatial agent, organization-wide aggregation, geometry, incidence, or hotspot detection.

The authenticated POST `/api/v1/health-sources/search` returns configured, approved source metadata only. `Ellai:HealthSources` defaults to an empty registry; each record must include an approved HTTPS URL on an exact allowed host, topic, jurisdiction, language, version, license, review time and expiry. Missing, expired, ambiguous or unapproved entries are excluded. The `community.health-research` interactive delegate and `ellai_approved_health_sources` tool return only this metadata and recheck it before publication and history replay; the delegate is enabled by default but remains unavailable when the registry is not configured. The registry does not fetch source text, generate medical advice or publish educational material. Operational approval, freshness and licensing of each configured source remain the deployer's responsibility.

The authenticated POST `/api/v1/community/research/feedback-aggregates` accepts owner-attested aggregate counts for a completed organization session and one of its existing topic tags. Both the feedback window and its end must be within the last 30 days. It rejects small cells and small complements, stores no individual responses, and binds submissions to the current owner, membership, actor epoch, organization, and session version. A submitted aggregate is not an approved research input until an organization owner records an accept decision through the review endpoint; self-review requires explicit acknowledgement and a protected reason. Correction, retrieval, and Ellai research integration are not implemented. Apply both community research migrations before enabling these endpoints. Counts are attestations, not verified population prevalence.
