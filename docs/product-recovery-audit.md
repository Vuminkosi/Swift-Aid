## Cross-cutting contracts and risks

- **No clinical capability:** Do not add or imply medical/veterinary advice, diagnosis, clinical triage, or generated health education. Preserve source evidence and unknown values; health-pattern inference remains off unless separately approved and validated.
- **Authorization and intake privacy:** Public intake does not create staff access or assignment. Preserve PC-09's minimum projection, versioned decisions and organization-review acceptance boundary. A chosen role/organization label is not an authorization grant.
- **Honest state:** Only a server-returned `IntakeReceipt` proves receipt. Draft/queued is local state. Fake services are gated development/test composition and cannot imply production persistence, assignment, consent, or delivery.
- **Backend dependencies:** Follow `docs/UI-BACKEND-HANDOFF.md`; typed frontend contracts are not approved wire APIs. Resolve request/response, auth, concurrency/idempotency, retry and error semantics before live adapters.
- **Voice and privacy:** Guest audio is encrypted and local-only in the documented Mobile path. No guest-media upload endpoint is evidenced; do not send local audio identifiers as uploaded attachment IDs. Actual device permission/capture/playback remains a separate check.
- **Ellai approval boundary:** Preserve explicit permission/processing grants and exact approval for privileged actions. Do not trust UI visibility, selected role or model output as authority. No hidden reasoning, raw prompts, secrets, health narratives or unauthorized data in UI context.
- **Design system:** MudBlazor is primary. AIS requires shared semantic tokens and light/dark coherence; use existing SwiftAid brand/theme adapters. Do not copy reference screenshot content or invent KPIs/actions.
