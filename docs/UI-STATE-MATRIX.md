## Required failure behavior

- `401` prompts the approved authentication flow; do not convert it into an empty success.
- `403` or generic `404` hides protected data and avoids existence disclosure where the endpoint contract makes denial and absence indistinguishable.
- `409` explains that state changed and offers a safe refresh/review path; do not replay a stale command blindly.
- `400` shows correctable input guidance; `413` explains size limits; `503` offers bounded retry where safe.
- Unknown or unavailable services remain visibly unavailable. Do not fabricate metrics, case IDs, timestamps, approvals or delivery receipts.

## Safety boundaries

SwiftAid does not provide medical/veterinary diagnosis, treatment advice, clinical triage, emergency dispatch or generated health education. Preserve original evidence and identify uncertain/missing facts. Optional cross-sector health-pattern inference remains disabled.
