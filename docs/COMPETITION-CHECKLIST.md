- [ ] Keep cross-sector health inference OFF unless the exact feature is clarified in writing; approvals never allow excluded advice.

## Working entry

- [ ] Install the release Android app; prove offline persistence, original audio and scoped synchronization.

- [ ] Record final build/commit hashes, demo access instructions, limitations and safe reset/restore evidence.
- [ ] Submit before November 4, 2026 at 9:00 PM Eastern (November 5 at 04:00 in Eswatini/Zimbabwe); capture actual platform receipt. Late submission is not assumed accepted.
